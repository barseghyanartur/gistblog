PhotoCraft feels like good old Photoshop

Published: Wed 07 October 2026
Updated: Sat 10 October 2026
By Artur Barseghyan

In Tech.

tags: photocraft photoshop image-editing open-source

I think this is just incredible. PhotoCraft feels like genuine Photoshop, but completely free.

So far, everything I've tested has worked well, with no bugs that I've noticed. Filters, Lens Correction, Layers, Curves, Hue/Saturation, Levels, Photo Filter, Filter Gallery.

It feels like the good old days, when Photoshop just worked. No AI, no monthly subscription. You owned the software, owned your content, and didn't have to share anything with anyone.

It's currently in early alpha, so do expect bugs. But again, overall, it's pretty incredible.
